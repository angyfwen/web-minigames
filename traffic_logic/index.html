how to play

1. look at the goal logic box to see the target sequence.

2. click the turn color blocks to choose signals.

3. add wait blocks to hold the signal state.

4. click run program to test your logic!

5. finish all 5 missions to win! (≧◡≦)

how is this educational?

1. sequencing: learn that the order of commands matters to reach a goal!

2. logic building: practice step-by-step thinking to solve complex problems.

3. patterns: recognize and recreate signals used in real-world systems.

4. computational thinking: break down a cycle into tiny, manageable instructions!
