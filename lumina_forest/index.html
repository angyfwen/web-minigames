educational value

lumina forest turns complex electronics into magic! (˶˃ ᵕ ˂˶)

✧
continuity: electricity needs a complete, unbroken loop to flow through the vines.
✧
switches: learn how control mechanisms (beetles) manage the flow of energy.
✧
resistance: use moss to "resist" powerful surges to protect components.
✧
parallel circuits: branch out paths to power multiple flowers at once.

how to play

restore the light to the ancient clearings! (˶˃ ᵕ ˂˶)

🌿
vines: tap gaps in the vine to bridge them and allow sap to flow.
🪲
beetles: tap beetles to wake them up. they act like switches!
🧽
glowing moss: some flowers are delicate. moss buffers the flow.
🌸
goal: connect the energy source to all flowers to light them up!
