How is this Educational?

Visualizing Fractions:
This game helps players connect abstract fractions (like 1/4) to concrete visual representations (pizza slices).

Division:
It reinforces the concept that cutting (dividing) an object into equal parts creates fractions.

Parts of a Whole:
Shows that 2 halves, 4 quarters, etc., make up one whole pizza.
