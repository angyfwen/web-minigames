how is this educational?

This game hones vital cognitive skills:

• Spatial Mapping: Translating cardinal directions into 360° rotation.
• Geometry: Practical understanding of degree increments and angles.
• Dexterity: Fine motor control through high-precision tactile interaction.

3. Hit Confirm to lock in your bearing!
